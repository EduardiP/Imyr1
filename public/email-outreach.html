Why reach new businesses?

A company that has just launched is choosing its tools, suppliers and partners right now. Reaching it early is the easiest way to find new business leads before your competitors do.

Every day we look for newly registered domains and websites that have just gone live. Each one is checked to be a real, working business website — not a parked or “coming soon” page — with a business email published on its own site. The ones that pass are added to the directory, next to established B2B companies, so you can find new businesses to contact in minutes.

Questions

Is the email outreach really free?

Yes. Every domain gets 3 free outreach emails, and no credit card is required.

Do I need my own contact list?

No. You pick recipients from the PhronexusAI directory of verified B2B companies, filtered by category, country and the year their domain was registered.

What counts as a “new business”?

Companies whose domain was registered or whose website went live in recent months. Each one is checked to be a real, working business website with a business email published on its own site. The directory also includes established companies.

Where do the email addresses come from?

Only from addresses a business publishes on its own website, on its own domain. Businesses that opt out are never contacted again.

Can I download the email list?

No. Emails are written and sent through PhronexusAI; the directory is not available for export.
