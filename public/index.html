Frequently Asked Questions

What you need to know

Does it cost anything?

Not to get started. Traffic exchange is free; you only pay if you choose Pro — and the first three months of Pro are free, no card required.

Will random ads show up on my site?

No. The network is SaaS-only, and matching is done with complementary businesses, not competitors.

How much space does it take on my site?

Zero when there's no promotion to show. When a promotion is displayed, the space takes its size and adapts to your page.

Is this for any kind of business?

The initial focus is SaaS companies, since they have access to code and benefit the most from complementary matching.
