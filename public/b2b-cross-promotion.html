What makes it built for B2B

Business audiences are small and specific. Showing the wrong ad to them — or a competitor's — costs more than it brings. That's why the network is built around four rules.

B2B meets B2B

B2B businesses are connected only with other B2B businesses. B2C and B2B2C have their own rules, because their visitors are different.

No competitors

Businesses that compete with you are identified and blocked before any ad is chosen. It's the network's core rule, not a setting.

AI audience match

AI compares category, subcategory and business description and gives every pair of businesses a 0–1000 score for how complementary their audiences are.

Your control

Approve every ad before it shows, allow only the categories you want and block specific businesses from your dashboard.

Two ways to share exposure

Pick the model that fits your goal and switch anytime, at no extra cost. Competitors stay blocked in both.

Weighted Auction · default

Impressions go to businesses by weight: the AI match score plus profile points earned from the impressions and verified conversions you give. Built for conversions.

Impression Balance

Get as many impressions as you give. The platform keeps what you give and receive as even as possible, and small differences are compensated automatically.

B2B cross-promotion: questions

What is B2B cross-promotion?

B2B cross-promotion is when two businesses that sell to other businesses, but don't compete with each other, show each other's ads to their own audiences. Each side reaches new, relevant customers without paying for ads.

Is the B2B cross-promotion platform free?

Yes. PhronexusAI has a permanent free plan with no credit card: up to 100 Auction and 100 Balance participations per day. The paid plan costs $7/month and raises the limit to 3,000 per day for each pool.

Will my competitors' ads appear on my website?

No. Before any ad is shown, the platform identifies businesses that compete with you and blocks them automatically. This is the network's core rule, not an optional setting.

Does it work for business websites that are not SaaS?

Yes. Any business with a website can join. B2B businesses are connected only with other B2B businesses, B2C with B2C, and B2B2C with both, because the visitors of each type are different.

Do I need technical skills to start?

No. You paste a small snippet into your site to open the ad space. An AI assistant shows you exactly where to place it and checks that it works. AI can also create your ads for you.

Can I control which ads appear on my site?

Yes. You can approve every ad before it shows, choose which categories are allowed and block specific businesses from your dashboard.
