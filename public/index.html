The Display System

Two display models. You choose.

The default model is the weighted auction — focused on conversions. If you prefer absolute balance, switch to the second model: get exactly as many impressions as you give. Change it anytime from your dashboard, at no extra cost.

How It Works

5 steps — from signup to your first display.

Steps 1–3 open as a guided walkthrough during signup. You can skip them, but we strongly recommend starting right away.

01AI Description

Register your business — AI generates the description

Give us your name and site URL. AI analyzes the page and automatically generates an accurate business description. Read it, edit it if you'd like, and approve it — this description is the foundation the platform uses to find complementary businesses.

02Segmentation

Set your type: B2B, B2C, or B2B2C

This step decides which businesses' websites your ad can appear on. B2B connects only with B2B, B2C only with B2C, B2B2C with everyone. The split is based on visitor type — the people visiting each type of site are fundamentally different.

03The Snippet

Add the display snippet to your site

Copy and paste a small block of code into your page — this opens the space where complementary businesses' ads will show. Claude AI is available as a direct assistant for the code installation: it tells you exactly where to place it and confirms everything works.

04Creatives

Create your ads in the Creatives section

The platform has its own Creatives section where you build your ads. Choose the format — static image, video (via YouTube URL), or interactive HTML5. For each format, you have two paths: upload your existing material, or let AI create the ad from scratch based on your business description.

05TrackingRecommended

Set up conversion tracking

Add the tracking snippet to your confirmation page, purchase button, or main site code. The platform measures every conversion coming from the network and automatically adds it as a point — increasing your weight in every future auction.

Platform

Every tool you need, built in.

Only complementary businesses. Competitors — automatically blocked.

After signup, the AI system compares your profile with every other business in the network. Category, subcategory, and business description are all considered. The result is a 0–1000 score showing how much your audience might interest the other business.

  • AI score of 0–1000 for every business pair — how complementary the audiences are
  • Connections only within the same type (B2B↔B2B, B2C↔B2C, B2B2C↔everyone)
  • Automatic identification and blocking of every competing business
  • Scores refresh with every new member in the network

AI creates all three formats. The platform saves them for you.

The Creatives section lets you build ads in three formats — and for each one, AI helps with the full creation: generating the image, structuring the HTML5, or helping with the video description.

  • Static image — AI generates it or upload your own material; saved for reuse
  • Video — give a YouTube URL; auto-embedded, no upload needed
  • Interactive HTML5 — AI generates animations and hover effects
  • Live preview of the ad before publishing

Three ways to measure every real conversion.

Tracking turns the platform from an exposure tool into a system with measurable results. The platform knows whether a visitor who took an action came directly from a network impression.

  • Method 1 — Confirmation page URL after purchase or signup
  • Method 2 — Snippet placed on the conversion button or function
  • Method 3 — Global snippet in the site's main code
  • The AI assistant confirms the installation is correct

What you gave. What you received. Who converted.

The analytics dashboard shows you everything, unfiltered: impressions given and received, your top-performing ads, verified conversions, and the categories of businesses you've connected with.

  • Direct comparison: impressions given vs. impressions received
  • Performance of every ad: clicks, CTR, and confirmed conversions
  • Active connection categories by auction weight
  • Profile points and growth projection

Precise permissions for every team member.

Businesses with larger teams can split access by function. Each member sees and does only what their role allows — no unnecessary access to sensitive settings.

  • Custom roles: ad creation, analytics, technical configuration
  • Every member is limited to their own role's functions
  • Full activity log for internal auditing
  • Available for businesses with complex organizational needs

Claude AI guides the installation of every snippet.

When you add the display or tracking snippet, Claude AI is available as a direct assistant right in the platform. It shows you exactly what code to copy and automatically confirms the connection works.

  • The display snippet — step-by-step guidance for your platform
  • The tracking snippet — installed on confirmation URL or button
  • Automatic verification after installation
  • Real-time assistance with no need for outside developers
