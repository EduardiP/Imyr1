How can SaaS and online businesses advertise for free?

SaaS and online businesses can advertise for free by swapping exposure with non-competing businesses, listing in the right directories, publishing helpful content, joining the communities where their customers already are, and contacting the right businesses directly. None of these cost money, but each costs time. This guide shows which methods work, which ones waste your effort, and how to do the hardest part automatically.

7 free ways to advertise that actually work

Ordered by how quickly they usually bring the first visitors to a new SaaS or online business.

1

Cross-promotion with non-competing businesses

Swap exposure with businesses that sell to the same customers but don't compete with you: your ad on their website, theirs on yours. Both sides reach people who already trust a related product, and nobody pays for clicks.

  • Best for: SaaS, agencies and any business with website traffic
  • Main cost: finding and agreeing with the right partners

2

Directories and launch platforms

List your product where people actively look for tools: Product Hunt, SaaS and AI directories, review sites with free listings, and Google Business Profile if you serve a local area.

  • Best for: new products that need first visitors and backlinks
  • Tip: choose a few relevant, well-known directories, not hundreds
3

Helpful content and SEO

Publish pages that answer the exact questions your customers ask, with real examples. Search engines and AI assistants like ChatGPT surface pages that answer clearly.

  • Best for: steady traffic over the long term
  • Expect: several months before results
4

Communities where your customers already are

Reddit, LinkedIn, Indie Hackers, Slack and Discord groups. Answer questions, share what you learned and mention your product only when it genuinely helps.

  • Best for: early feedback and first users
  • Rule: give value first; promotion comes second
5

Personal B2B email outreach

Contact businesses that could genuinely use your product with short, personal emails, a few per day, using public business addresses and a clear way to opt out.

  • Best for: B2B products with a clear customer profile
  • Respect the rules: CAN-SPAM, GDPR and an unsubscribe link
6

Integrations and app marketplaces

If your product connects with other tools, list it in their marketplaces and plugin directories. Users discover you inside tools they already use.

  • Best for: SaaS that works alongside other software
  • Benefit: you appear where buyers search for solutions
7

Founder-led social and building in public

Share progress, lessons and real numbers on LinkedIn or X. People follow stories, and a consistent presence turns into trust and sign-ups.

  • Best for: founders willing to post consistently
  • Expect: slow start, compounding results

Free advertising tactics that don't work

These look free and fast, but they usually bring no customers, waste time or put your website and accounts at risk.

✕ Buying backlinks or joining link exchanges

Links bought or swapped only to manipulate rankings break Google's spam policies. They can be ignored or lead to a penalty, and they bring no real customers.

✕ Traffic exchanges and auto-surf sites

Visits come from people who browse only to earn credits, not from buyers. Conversions stay near zero, and the fake traffic distorts your analytics.

✕ Spamming communities and DMs

Dropping links in every group or sending the same pitch to strangers gets you banned and damages your brand where your future customers spend time.

✕ Blasting purchased email lists

Bought lists contain old, unverified and often personal addresses. Bounces and spam complaints can get your domain blocked, and in many countries it breaks the law.

✕ Fake reviews and paid upvotes

Platforms remove fake reviews and ban accounts, and in the US the FTC has banned fake reviews and testimonials. One discovered fake review costs more trust than it ever brings.

✕ Hundreds of low-quality directories

Mass submissions to directories nobody visits bring almost no traffic, and search engines ignore most of those links.

✕ Mass-produced AI pages

Publishing many near-identical pages just to rank is what Google calls scaled content abuse. It can lower the visibility of your whole website.

Free advertising for SaaS: questions

Is there really such a thing as free advertising?

Yes, but it is never free of effort. The methods on this page cost little or no money, and you pay with time instead: writing content, building partnerships or joining communities. Some tools, like PhronexusAI, offer a free plan that removes most of the manual work.

What is the fastest free way to get visitors to a new SaaS?

Directories, launch platforms and cross-promotion usually bring the first visitors fastest, often within days. SEO and content take longer, typically several months, but keep bringing traffic over time.

Does cross-promotion work for B2B SaaS?

Yes, when the partners share an audience and don't compete. A tool your customers already use is a trusted place to be seen. The key is matching: the wrong partner brings visitors who never convert.

Are traffic exchanges and auto-surf sites worth it?

Usually not. They generate visits from people who browse only to earn credits, not from potential customers. Those visits rarely convert and can distort your analytics.
