1. The Service

PhronexusAI is a cross-promotion network for SaaS businesses. Each participating business installs a small snippet on their own website; in exchange, that space is used to display promotions from other participating businesses, and their own promotions are shown on other participants' properties in return.

PhronexusAI offers two distribution mechanisms, either or both of which a business may use for a given ad space: Auction, where placement is determined by a weighted-random selection reflecting relevance and reciprocity, and Balance, where placement is determined by evening out how much exposure a business has given versus received. A business may choose either mode manually, or allow the platform to allocate space automatically between the two based on its own settings.

Businesses may also restrict which categories of other businesses they are willing to exchange exposure with, using the category-restriction controls available in their account.
