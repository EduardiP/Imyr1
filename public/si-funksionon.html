How It Works

The full mechanism, nothing hidden

PhronexusAI is a cross-promotion network — SaaS businesses within the network promote each other reciprocally, instead of paid ads. It exists for one purpose: to give your SaaS business real exposure to a warm, complementary audience — with no ad budget, and never exposing you to your own competitors. This page describes the exact mechanism, formulas, and logic — not general marketing talk.

The core guarantee: never your competitors

During signup, your business's category and subcategory are stored. Every time the system chooses who shows on your page, businesses in your category are automatically excluded — before any other calculation even begins. Plus, you have extra control: you can exclude any other category you're not interested in.

Step 1 — before anything else

Which pool is used: Auction or Balance?

This is the first decision, made before any other calculation. Every time someone visits your page, the system decides which of the 2 pools this specific impression falls into.

Manual Mode

You set the ratio yourself (e.g. 70% Auction / 30% Balance) from your dashboard. The system follows that exact ratio, generation after generation.

Automatic Mode

The platform decides for itself, per impression, which pool to use — based on a fairness mechanism that tracks a running balance between the two pools (details below), not a fixed ratio.

Automatic Mode — the exact mechanism

How the platform decides, step by step

1

A running global balance

The platform tracks a single running number: how many impressions the Auction pool "owes" the Balance pool, or vice versa. This is separate from your own personal Balance deficit — it's a network-wide fairness counter between the two pools themselves.

2

The ±10 limit

If this running balance reaches 10 in either direction, the next impression is routed directly to whichever pool is owed — no further calculation, no randomness. This guarantees neither pool is ever starved for more than a handful of impressions in a row.

3

Paying the debt down, one step at a time

Each time this direct routing happens, the running balance moves 1 step back toward zero (e.g. 10 → 9) — not reset to zero all at once. After enough consecutive direct-routings, the balance falls back under the limit and normal weighted competition resumes.

4

Below the limit: top 5 from each pool

When the running balance is under the limit, the system pulls the top 5 candidates by weight from the Auction pool, and the top 5 from the Balance pool, separately.

5

A sharpening formula

Each candidate's weight is passed through (3/40000)x² − (3/200)x + 7/4 — a curve that widens the gap between strong and weak candidates (e.g. weight 100 → 1 point, weight 900 → 49 points) without ever reducing anyone to exactly zero.

6

Pool vs pool, not business vs business

The 5 transformed scores from each pool are summed into one total per pool. The final choice — Auction or Balance — is a weighted lottery between these two pool totals, not between individual businesses directly.

Step 2A — if it falls to the Auction

How the auction weight is calculated

1

AI Score (matching)

When you sign up, AI compares your business's description/category with every other business of the same type (B2B↔B2B, B2C↔B2C; B2B2C connects with everyone). For every pair, a numeric score is stored — how complementary the audiences are.

2

Profile Points

Exact formula: real views given ÷ RATE + conversions, calculated from the last 30 days only (a rolling window — older activity naturally ages out). A "real view" requires at least 50% of the ad visible for 1+ second, not just a load. RATE varies by type — 175 for B2B, 320 for B2C, 248 for B2B2C. Every verified conversion adds 1 point directly.

3

New-business boost

If you're among the top 3 combinations by AI score for an advertiser, you get an initial "boost" — only when AI points are between 20–320. The boost is subtracted automatically from your profile points — the more active you become, the faster it fades on its own.

Total weight = AI Score + Profile Points + Boost (if applicable)

Win probability = your weight ÷ the sum of weights of all qualifying candidates (weighted lottery, not a fixed ranking).

Step 2B — if it falls to Balance

How the Balance model decides

Like the Auction, Balance is a weighted lottery — not a fixed ranking. The difference is what drives the weight: fairness (what you've given vs received), not raw performance.

1

Competitors are excluded first

A candidate is removed from the running entirely if their AI matching score is 0 AND they're the same business type as you (B2B↔B2B or B2C↔B2C). If the AI score is 0 but the types differ, they stay in — that's just a neutral match, not a competitor.

2

Your deficit is calculated

For each remaining candidate: impressions you've given them − impressions you've received from them (Balance pool only). A negative deficit means you've received more than you've given; positive means the opposite.

3

The deficit becomes a bonus or a penalty

A negative deficit (you've received more) adds a growing bonus to their AI score — the more negative, the bigger the bonus, uncapped. A positive deficit (you've given more) subtracts a growing penalty instead, floored at zero.

Final score = max(0, AI score + deficit-based bonus/penalty)

Win probability = your final score ÷ the sum of final scores of all remaining candidates — a weighted lottery, same principle as the Auction. If only 1 candidate survives the competitor-exclusion step, they win directly with no further calculation.

Step 3 — after the winning business

Which specific ad shows

Here's another, separate decision from Step 1 — once the winning business is determined (from either the Auction or Balance), if they have 2 or more active ads, which one specifically to show must be decided.

A

New ad (<5 views)

Gets automatic rotating priority — every new ad goes through a "data collection" phase before real performance starts deciding.

B

2 ads, both with history

Weighted lottery based on performance scores (views+conversions) of each — not a fixed 50/50, the higher-performing one has better odds, but no guarantee.

C

3+ active ads

The same weighted-lottery logic extends across all ads at once — each participates proportionally to its own score, regardless of the total count. On top of this, the same visitor won't see the same ad twice in a row within one visit — each refresh or page shows a different one, cycling through all available ads before repeating.

4

Snippet & real placement

Add one line of code to your page. This does two things at once: enters your business into the auction pool, and opens the real space where an ad shows. Takes up zero space when there's no ad to display.

5

AI-created ad formats

Three formats: image, video (YouTube link), or interactive HTML5. Upload your own, or let AI create it from scratch based on your business description.

6

Tracking & Analytics

Set up conversion tracking (confirmation URL, button snippet, or global) and see everything on your dashboard: impressions given/received, per-ad performance, verified conversions.

That's it — no third parties, no ad budget

The more exposure/conversions you give, the higher you naturally rank in the auction — and your competitors never show, in either pool.
