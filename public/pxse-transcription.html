How it works

Three steps. A few minutes.

Paste your links

Open the tool, sign in or create a free Apify account, and add your audio or video links or a podcast feed.

Choose options

Language (or auto-detect), speaker labels, AI summary and the files you want: TXT, SRT, VTT, Markdown, JSON.

Download your files

Click Start. When it finishes, open the Output tab and download your transcript and subtitles.
